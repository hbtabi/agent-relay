# Launch plan for a London AI video studio

## Audience
- Define two primary personas for a London AI video studio, with budgets and buying triggers
- Validate both with five short customer interviews
- Prioritise the persona with the shortest sales cycle

## Go-to-market
- Short-form vertical video outperforms static posts for discovery
- Pair organic posting about a London AI video studio with a small paid retargeting budget
- Use case-study carousels for consideration-stage buyers

## Risks & mitigations
- Scope creep: fix deliverables and revision counts up front
- Capacity: template repeatable work before scaling sales
- Cash flow: take a 50% deposit on new projects
